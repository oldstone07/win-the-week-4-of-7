Privacy Policy

Win the Week

Last updated · May 5, 2026

Win the Week is built on a simple promise: your notes are yours. This page explains what that means in practice, what the app does and does not do with your data, and who has access to what.

What you write stays yours

The notes, reflections, weekly questions, and daily Win/Loss outcomes you log in Win the Week are stored on your device using Apple's standard storage. We do not collect, read, or have access to any of this content. No one at our end can see what you wrote.

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data syncs across your own devices using Apple's iCloud service. This sync is end-to-end encrypted by Apple. Your notes pass through Apple's servers in encrypted form so they appear on your other devices, but Apple cannot read them and we cannot read them.

No AI reads your notes. No third party sees them. They are yours alone.

What the app does not do

  • The app does not include analytics or behavioral tracking. We do not know which screens you visit, how often you open the app, or what you tap.
  • The app does not include advertising of any kind.
  • The app does not send your notes, reflections, or questions to any AI service for analysis, summarization, or any other purpose.
  • The app does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, ever.
  • The app does not require an account or sign-in. There is no profile to create.

Third-party services

Win the Week uses two third-party services for technical operation. Neither has access to your notes or reflections.

Apple iCloud handles optional sync between your own devices. Sync is end-to-end encrypted by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to data stored in iCloud and is available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

RevenueCat handles in-app purchases for the optional Pro upgrade. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information so the purchase can be validated and your Pro access can be unlocked across your devices. RevenueCat does not receive your notes, reflections, or any content you write in the app. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders in the app, notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Apple's local notification system. No data is sent to a server to schedule a reminder. The reminder text is generic and does not include any of your notes.

Your data, your choices

Because your data is stored locally and synced through your own iCloud account, you have full control over it at all times.

  • Edit anything, anytime. Past days, past notes, past reflections, past Win/Loss outcomes. The app treats your records as yours to revise.
  • Export your data. The app provides a JSON export feature so you can save a personal backup. The export is created on your device and never sent anywhere automatically.
  • Delete your data. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from that device. To remove iCloud-synced copies, you can delete the app's data from iCloud through your device's iCloud settings.

Children's privacy

Win the Week is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us using the email below.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be communicated within the app.
